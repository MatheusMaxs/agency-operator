# Legal And Commercial Rules

This is not legal advice. Use a responsible adult/legal guardian and qualified professional where needed.

Minimum safe rules:

- If the operator is under 18, an adult/legal guardian must handle Stripe, contracts, invoices, taxes, and business ownership.
- Outreach must be transparent and include an easy opt-out.
- Do not claim a business requested work if it did not.
- Do not imply partnership with Google, Meta, directories, or the target business.
- Do not scrape private data.
- Store source URLs for public information.
- Respect applicable anti-spam and privacy rules.
- Remove a lead from outreach immediately after opt-out.
- Do not generate fake testimonials, fake reviews, fake credentials, or fake awards.
- Do not publish final sites without explicit business approval.

Email wording must include:

- Who is contacting them.
- Why they are receiving the email.
- That the preview is a concept.
- How to opt out.

Payment rules:

- Use Stripe or another legitimate processor.
- Do not take payment without clear package terms.
- Store package, amount, currency, status, and invoice link.
- Deliver only what was promised.
